## pattern.css — selected background formulas

The Gingham, Chevron, Triangles and Cross dots patterns, and related line/grid/dot
formulas in `src/patterns.js`, are adapted from **pattern.css 1.0.0** by **bansal-io**.
They are parameterized for this application's colors, opacity, and repeat sizes.
Linen and Notebook combine/adapt the line formulas. The complete library is not
installed or required at runtime.
