Help

A few tips for managing your lists.

Reorder tasks

Drag a task’s grip to move it within its list. With a keyboard, focus the grip and press ↑ or ↓. Press Escape to cancel a drag. Clear search before reordering.

Your lists stay in this browser

Lists are saved automatically on this device and browser. They do not sync to other devices. Clearing browser data can remove them.

Keep a backup

Choose Export JSON in the sidebar to download a copy of your lists. Use Import JSON to restore a backup. Import asks for confirmation before replacing your current lists.

Deadline warnings

A yellow triangle means a deadline is within 3 days. A red octagon means today or tomorrow. A red clock and soft red background mean overdue. Completed items have no warnings.

Edit a list or task

Open a list’s ••• menu and choose Edit list to change its name, appearance, or deadline. Choose Edit beside a task to update its text, priority, or due date.

Sidebar quotes

Quotes change every five minutes while this tab is visible. Choose Next quote for another, or Pause to stop rotation. Quotable supplies motivational and humorous quotes when available; original app quotes work offline. No task data is sent to the quote service.
